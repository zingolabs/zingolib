# Pool Activation is derived once, in pepper-sync, from zcash_protocol parameters

The height at which a shielded pool begins to exist (Sapling at the Sapling upgrade, Orchard at NU5, Ironwood at NU6.3) was hand-rolled independently in zingolib's witness guard and pepper-sync's scan-range clamp, and was absent entirely from the migration schedule planner, which let a pre-activation consent schedule every part below the NU6.3 activation (issue #2493, finding 6). Upstream `zcash_protocol` deliberately ships `ShieldedPool` and `NetworkUpgrade` as unrelated enums, so the mapping between them is ours to define, and we decided it is defined exactly once: in `pepper_sync::wallet`, as the Pool Activation derivation over `zcash_protocol::consensus::Parameters`, preserving `Option` because a chain that never activates the upgrade is a real state. Every height clamp involving a pool's existence (the witness guard's clamp of the wallet Birthday to the Pool Activation, the scan-range clamp, the migration schedule's activation floor) is a `max()` against this one derivation; a second pool-to-upgrade mapping anywhere in the workspace is a defect.

Naming policy, ratified with this decision: **Birthday belongs to wallets alone.** A wallet has a Birthday; a network upgrade has an activation height; a pool has its Pool Activation, the activation height of the upgrade that introduces it. No pool or upgrade concept is ever named a birthday (the retired `effective_pool_birthday` conflated the two), and no activation concept is named a "start."

## Considered Options

`zingo_common_components` looks adjacent because `ActivationHeights` lives there, but it is a deliberately zcash-crate-free mirror-vocabulary layer maintained in a separate repository: it has neither `ShieldedPool` nor `NetworkUpgrade` nor the `Parameters` trait, so hosting the mapping there would force it either to grow a `zcash_protocol` dependency (defeating its purpose) or to mirror two more enums and tax every consumer with boundary conversions, against the standing discipline of converting mirrored types only at the `zcash_local_net` boundary, and every refinement would require cross-repo publish-and-repin choreography. Defining the mapping in zingolib alone would leave pepper-sync's copy standing, and deliberate duplication with an agreement test is the disease being cured. pepper-sync is the only home every consumer reaches without any dependency change: it already speaks `zcash_protocol` natively, zingolib already depends on it, and its ratified shrink-only dependency rule stays intact.

## Consequences

zingolib's witness-guard scan floor and pepper-sync's inline scan-range mapping become derivations from the single source, and the migration schedule planner gains the activation floor it lacked. The migration modules' broadcast-decoupling lint is unaffected: `pepper_sync::wallet` is already their one permitted pepper-sync surface.
